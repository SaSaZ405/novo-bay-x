# NOVO-BAY(X): GIS & RS Spatial Analytics Module

## Overview
The GIS & Remote Sensing (RS) panel converts raw communication vectors into spatial intelligence for decision-makers and enterprise clients.

## Core Features
1. **Real-time Geographic Audience Heatmaps:** Dynamic visualization of active user clusters across regions.
2. **Spatial Supply Chain & Logistics Tracking:** Interactive map routing for cross-border physical assets and products.
3. **Location-Based Crisis Management:** Auto-routing communications via alternative channels during localized network disruptions.
4. **Spatial Market Intelligence:** Geospatial overlays combining audience density with economic indicators.
